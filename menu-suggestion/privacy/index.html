Privacy Policy

Clear about what stays on your device.

Effective date: August 18, 2026. This policy explains the data handling of Menu Suggestion AI by DGY Creative Lab.

On-device data

Menu prompts, generated menu suggestions, preferences, Favorites, and recommendation history are processed or stored on the device. The developer does not receive them.

No account or advertising SDK

The current iOS app does not require an account and does not include a remote LLM, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK.

Location, weather, and maps

Optional location

The app may ask for when-in-use location permission at launch to personalize weather. Denying permission does not disable menu recommendation.

Weather request

When permission and a location are available, coordinates are used in a weather request. WeatherKit is tried first; Open-Meteo may be used only as a fallback. The app does not keep location history.

Nearby restaurants

Apple MapKit is used only after you choose Find nearby restaurants. It searches real places around a current location or an area you enter. You can open a result in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

WeatherKit, Open-Meteo, Apple Maps, and Google Maps process those requests under their own policies. This app does not send prompt text, Favorites, or recommendation history with weather or map-search requests. If you choose Google Maps, the restaurant name and coordinates are sent to Google to open that place.

Your choices

  • Change location access at any time in iOS, iPadOS, or macOS settings.
  • Delete Favorites and recommendation history inside the app.
  • Do not grant location permission; core menu recommendation remains available.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when app behavior, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated with a revised policy.
