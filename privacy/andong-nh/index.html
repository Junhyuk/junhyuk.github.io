4. Optional Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

This section applies only from the first Google Play version of package com.jun.andongNHFruit2 that includes the Firebase integration. Analytics and diagnostic sharing are separate choices, each off by default, and can be enabled or withdrawn in the app’s privacy settings. The app’s core features remain available when either choice is off. These settings are separate from the existing advertising settings.

Usage analytics

When Analytics is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase may process app launches and sessions, app and device information, an app instance identifier, and a coarse region inferred from the IP address. App-defined events use fixed event names and categories. The app does not put free-text searches, detailed route, stop or item IDs, favorites, precise GPS location, URLs, names, email addresses, phone numbers or account IDs in Firebase event values. Firebase Advertising ID collection is disabled, and Firebase advertising storage, advertising user data and personalized advertising consent remain denied. This does not change what AdMob or other existing advertising services may process.

Error diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics may process a crash stack trace, app state and version, operating-system and device information, an installation identifier, and related session diagnostics. An unsent crash report may be stored temporarily on the device even when diagnostic sharing is off. Automatic upload is disabled. The app requests upload only when diagnostics has been separately enabled and the saved prior consent and report state meet the sending conditions. If permitted prior consent cannot be verified for a report, the app does not request upload and asks the SDK to delete it. Enabling diagnostics does not immediately send every existing report.

Withdrawal, local storage and retention

After withdrawal, the app blocks requests for new app-defined Analytics events and asks Analytics to stop collection and deny consent. Turning diagnostics off closes the app’s future sharing conditions and requests deletion of unsent reports. The app cannot immediately cancel SDK processing or a network transfer already in progress, and it cannot confirm that deletion or transmission has completed. A transfer already requested may finish after withdrawal.

Choices and unsent reports may be stored on the device and read again at the next launch. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove that device’s app storage, but does not guarantee deletion of information already sent to Google or cancellation of a transfer in progress. Google’s service policies and the actual service settings govern retention and deletion; this policy states no unverified retention period or completed-deletion guarantee. See the common Firebase addendum and Firebase privacy information for further details.
